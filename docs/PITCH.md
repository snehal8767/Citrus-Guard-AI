# Pitch (60 seconds)

"Vidarbha grows India's most famous oranges, but a farmer with fifty acres still spots disease by walking the rows — days late — then sprays the whole orchard just in case.

CitrusGuardAI closes that loop: scan the orchard, detect stress per zone with AI, score the risk transparently, alert the farmer, and act precisely — six acres treated instead of fifty. And the AI can never spray on its own: every intervention needs the farmer's tap, enforced by the server.

It's live today — full-stack, tested, repeatable demo — and it's honest about what it is: a prototype with a synthetic model and simulated drone, built so a real field model and real sensors drop straight in. We're looking for pilot orchards and a plant-pathology partner to validate it this season."
